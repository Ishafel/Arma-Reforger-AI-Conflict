# 0.1.15 — Supply deliveries and map markers

- **Manual supply deliveries:** choose a source base, an allied destination
  and the supply amount through the new supply menu. Player requests replace
  automatic dispatch for both factions.
- **Delivery tracking:** the supply panel and vehicle marker show the route,
  status, cargo and estimated arrival time for the current leg.
- **More resilient logistics:** improved vehicle spawning and departure.
  Stuck vehicles can relocate to a clear road position, and drivers can return
  to their seats while preserving the cargo and assigned job.
- **Squad and logistics markers:** a unified compact style, panels that fit
  their text and additional details on hover.
- **UI fixes:** resolved map-opening and supply-form hover errors, and fixed
  references to stock UI resources.

Arrival times are approximate and update as vehicles move or stop.
Server tests still report AI and vehicle cleanup errors; full join-in-progress
(JIP) and visual validation remain pending. See the
[supply system report](../SUPPLY_MAP_UI.md) for details.
