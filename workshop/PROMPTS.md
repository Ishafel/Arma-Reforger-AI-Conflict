# ImageGen briefs для Workshop preview

Обе финальные обложки созданы встроенным ImageGen и сохранены как квадратные
JPEG без текста, логотипов, insignia и watermarks. PNG-оригиналы вне
репозитория не являются release assets; canonical файлы — `preview.jpg` в
каталогах addon.

## AI Conflict Core

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square Workshop preview image for a game systems dependency mod
Primary request: a map-independent autonomous Cold War battlefield command system, conveying opposing AI forces coordinating across a tactical radio network
Scene/backdrop: dark topographic command-map surface with subtle contour lines and a branching radio-node graph
Subject: two opposing formations represented by small infantry and ground-vehicle silhouettes, one side cool steel blue and the other muted Soviet red, converging toward central objectives
Style/medium: polished cinematic military strategy key art, stylized realism, clean strong shapes readable as a small thumbnail
Composition/framing: square, symmetrical high-angle composition, central objective and radio graph as focal point, generous safe margins
Lighting/mood: restrained dramatic glow, tense and analytical rather than explosive
Color palette: charcoal, desaturated olive, cool steel blue, muted red, warm off-white map markings
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no flags, no insignia, no logos, no trademarks, no watermark, no gore; do not depict a specific real-world island or named location
Avoid: photorealistic game screenshot, modern drones, science-fiction HUD clutter, tiny illegible UI elements
```

## AI Conflict Arland

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square Workshop preview image for an Arma Reforger Cold War game-mode integration mod
Primary request: autonomous US-versus-USSR combined-arms conflict unfolding across a rugged North Atlantic island, with radio-connected objectives and an active frontline
Scene/backdrop: windswept green island terrain with rocky coast, sparse pine woods, small rural settlements, narrow roads and a cold gray sea; evoke a fictional 1980s North Atlantic island without copying a specific map layout
Subject: opposing infantry squads and period-appropriate tracked and wheeled military vehicles advancing from opposite sides toward radio towers and contested bases; tactical movement is the focus, not individual hero characters
Style/medium: polished cinematic military strategy key art, stylized realism, faithful late-Cold-War atmosphere
Composition/framing: square oblique aerial establishing view, island coastline readable, central contested radio tower and branching radio links as focal point, strong thumbnail silhouette, safe margins
Lighting/mood: overcast Atlantic daylight breaking into restrained warm light over the central objective, tense large-scale campaign mood
Color palette: natural dark greens, slate sea and sky, weathered earth, restrained blue and muted red tactical accents
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no flags, no insignia, no logos, no trademarks, no watermark, no gore, no modern equipment, no science-fiction interface
Avoid: photorealistic game screenshot, recognizable real map geometry, excessive explosions, crowded tiny details
```
